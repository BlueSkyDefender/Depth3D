Getting started

  1. Install ReShade for your game.
  2. Put the Depth3D shaders in your shader folder.
  3. Launch the game and press Home to open the ReShade menu.
  4. Tick SuperDepth3D.

Overwatch comes with Depth3D and configures most games for you, so there is often nothing left to adjust.

Rather not do it by hand? GPU Selector installs ReShade, Depth3D and the add-ons for you.

Game compatibility

PCGamingWiki keeps the compatibility list. Look for Depth Map Compatibility. This works on both AMD and NVIDIA GPUs.

No profile for your game? The shader will say so, and the Shader Adjustments page shows you how to build one. Send profiles my way and I will add them.
