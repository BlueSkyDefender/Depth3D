How the shaders are licensed today

Depth3D is free for personal use. It is not open source, and it is not free to ship inside a product.

The shaders are proprietary. You are welcome to download them and use them yourself, on your own machine, for as long as you like. Redistribution needs my authorization, and that includes bundling them with hardware, putting them in a driver or utility, or shipping them as part of anything you sell.

That is not there to catch people out. It exists so that when a manufacturer wants to use this properly, we can have a real conversation about it.

What a licence usually covers

Every arrangement is different, but these are the usual pieces:

Shipping it with your hardware
Bundling Depth3D with a device, a driver package or your own utility, so it is there when the customer opens the box.
Tuning for your panel
A display behaves the way it behaves. Interlace pattern, subpixel layout, lens geometry, view count. The output can be matched to it rather than approximated.
Per game profiles
Overwatch already carries profiles for a large number of games. That work can be aimed at the titles that matter to your launch.
Ongoing support
Games update, engines change and upscalers move the depth buffer around. Keeping a device working over time is its own job.
